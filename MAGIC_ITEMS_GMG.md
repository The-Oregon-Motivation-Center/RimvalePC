## Common Magic Items

Common Magic Items
Thes have been created many times and can be found at magic merchant shops.

Effect: Grants advantage on Divinity checks made to identify or interact with divine relics.

Effect: Basic action to apply to stabilize a dying creature and grant 1 HP.
Dagger of the Last Word (Common)
A ceremonial dagger with a whispering edge.

## Uncommon Magic Items

Uncommon Magic items
These items have been created multiple times and can be found fairly easily with a little bit of work and effort.

This sword does +1 to attack and damage.
Aberrant Flex Band (Uncommon)
A flexible band that warps the wearer’s form.
Effect: Once per short rest, move through spaces as narrow as 6 inches without squeezing. As a free action, increase melee reach by 5 feet, while active max AP is reduced by 2.

An amulet that allows the attuned to conjure an inanimate construct in the form of a simple weapon of your choice, it follows standard inanimate construct rules and has an HP of 15 and can be conjured once per long rest. It can be stowed into and retrieved from a pocket dimension as an action and can be equipped as a part of the action of retrieving it from the pocket dimension. If a ranged weapon is chosen, either ammo can be added or it generates ammo that does force damage.

A modular arcane limb replacement forged from enchanted alloys and runes.
Effect: The wearer may replace a lost limb with this core, gaining basic functionality. As a free action, the limb can extend up to 5 feet for utility tasks (not attacks) for 2 AP.

A silver circlet set with a pale blue gem, worn by the secretive keepers of the vault.
Effect: Grants advantage on Speechcraft checks to negotiate or keep secrets. Once per long rest, you may cast Detect Magic (1 SP version) for 1 minute without expending SP.
Armorweaver’s Thread (Uncommon)
A spool of metallic thread infused with protective magic.
Effect: Sew into light or medium armor to restore 2d6 HP and grant +1 AC for 1 hour. Usable once per long rest.
Automaton Servant Token (Uncommon)
A small, clockwork token that summons a minor automaton servant.
Effect: 1 SP to activate, summon an automaton for 1 hour. The automaton can perform simple tasks like cooking, cleaning, delivering messages, or serving as a discreet spy. It can speak Bable. It cannot fight for you but will run away to the best of its abilities when threatened. AC 10, HP 5, foot speed of 30 and it has advantage on Stealth related checks in urban environments.

Bag of Tricks (Uncommon)
This bag, made from gray, rust, or tan cloth, appears empty. However, reaching inside reveals the presence of a small, fuzzy object.
Usage: You can take a Magic action to pull a fuzzy object from the bag and throw it up to 20 feet. When the object lands, it transforms into a creature determined by rolling on the table that corresponds to the bag’s color (see below). Refer to “Animals” for the creature’s stat block. The creature vanishes at the next dawn or when reduced to 0 Hit Points.
The creature is friendly to you and your allies, acting immediately after you on your initiative count. You can command how the creature moves and what action it takes on its next turn (such as attacking an enemy). If you give no orders, the creature acts according to its nature.
You can build the stats and abilities based on the Animal creator rules for the danger level of the animal.
Once three fuzzy objects have been pulled from the bag, it can’t be used again until the next dawn.
